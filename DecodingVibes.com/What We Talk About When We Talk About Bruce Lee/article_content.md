Recently, I spent an entire afternoon watching YouTube reels of Bruce Lee performing martial arts in slow motion, with voice-over of his quotes. In some videos, he’s doing really strong kicks while talking about how we should be like water, and in others, he’s engaged in a very intense conversation, saying things directly instead of using a voice-over. 

Only once the afternoon had fully passed, and I'd watched what felt like over thirty short videos, did I realize they were all (or at least the majority of them) completely AI-generated. 

When I asked Google Gemini 3.1 Pro Mode (known for its deep thinking abilities) about this, it gave me the typical “You’re not crazy” response, assuring me that it was becoming a pretty common tactic on YouTube these days to create such videos and waste the time of otherwise totally-employed and useful people like me, for views and dollars. 

But okay, so what?

The reason why this felt like a teachable moment was not that I had been duped by AI videos. If created properly, AI video is extremely good today, and within certain parameters (though it is still massively expensive for most use cases). 

But rather the simple question - why Bruce Lee? 

AI could have been used to generate anything, but whoever created those videos deliberately chose Bruce Lee as the subject. And the best part was that it totally worked! I was hooked on those videos. Bruce Lee felt like a mythical being, as if he were somehow interacting with the force on planet Earth. And his philosophy? I had never wished to be water more (even though all humans are mostly water by definition). 

And this made me really think - where did the whole “Bruce Lee as a philosopher” meme emerge in the first place? Was there any truth to the idea, or was it just a caricature or helpful image propagated by media firms?

A bigger and more important goal emerged in my mind - I wanted to find out if the idea of Bruce Lee that those AI-generated reels sold to me and the idea that has arguably been sold by Hollywood for decades, was real or not. 

Who was Bruce Lee, the actual man, and what was the meme that his image and personal brand became completely attached to, and why does the idea of him (the meme of it all) still exist in the cultural zeitgeist today?
