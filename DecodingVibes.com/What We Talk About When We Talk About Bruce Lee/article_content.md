I recently spent an entire afternoon watching YouTube reels of Bruce Lee performing martial arts in slow motion. In some videos, he’s doing really strong kicks while talking about how we should be like water, and in others, he’s engaged in a very intense conversation, saying things directly instead of using a voiceover. 

Only once the afternoon had fully passed, and I'd watched what felt like over thirty short videos, did I realize they were all (or at least the majority of them) completely AI-generated. 

When I asked Google Gemini 3.1 Pro Mode (known for its deep thinking abilities) about this, it gave me the typical “You’re not crazy” response, assuring me that it was becoming a pretty common tactic on YouTube these days to create such videos and waste the time of otherwise totally-employed and useful people like me, for views and dollars. 

But okay, so what?

The reason why this felt like a teachable moment was not that I had been duped by AI videos. If generated properly, AI video is extremely good today, and within certain parameters (though it is still massively expensive for most use cases).

But rather the simple question rang in my mind - why Bruce Lee?

AI could have been used to generate anything, but whoever created those videos deliberately chose Bruce Lee as the subject. 

And the best part was that it totally worked! I was hooked on those videos. Bruce Lee felt like a mythical being, as if he were somehow interacting with the force on planet Earth. And his philosophy? I had never wished to be water more (even though all humans are mostly water by definition). 

And this made me really think - where did the whole “Bruce Lee as a philosopher” meme emerge in the first place? Was there any truth to the idea, or was it just a caricature or helpful image propagated by media firms?

I wanted to find out if the idea of Bruce Lee that those AI-generated reels sold to me and the idea that has arguably been sold by Hollywood for decades, was real or not. 

Who was Bruce Lee, the actual man, and what was the meme that his image and personal brand became completely attached to, and why does the idea of him (the meme of it all) still exist in the cultural zeitgeist today? 

Bruce Lee and the philosophy of punching

From a strictly academic perspective, Bruce Lee does not stand a chance of being considered as having made any contributions to the field of philosophy. While you can easily find numerous citations of work by contemporary thinkers like Thomas Nagel in academia, nobody is really citing Bruce Lee.

So it is clear that he wasn’t a philosopher in the strictly academic sense. We do not need to look very deeply to understand why this is the case.

However, the point of this article is much broader than just the question, “Would you consider Bruce Lee to be qualified to talk about philosophy in an academic sense?” 

The point is to examine the image he curated, the reasons it worked, and why it continues to resonate with people to this very day.

To do this, we must take a couple of things into consideration, such as:

What is an objective description of this image? 
Bruce Lee’s life, sudden passing, and how it affected the image and its everlasting quality.
The idea of art versus philosophy, and how we can get lost in the aesthetics of knowledge rather than the actual subject itself, ultimately drains us of a richer life.

What was/is the Bruce Lee Image?
